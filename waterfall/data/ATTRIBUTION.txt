Place names in Waterfall
========================

places.tsv.gz and countries.tsv in this folder are derived from the GeoNames
geographical database:

    GeoNames            https://www.geonames.org/
    Licence             Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
                        https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
    Source files        https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/
                        (one of the "cities" lists, countryInfo.txt and, in
                        some copies, whole-country files)

Changes made to the GeoNames data: only populated places are kept; districts
inside a city and abandoned, destroyed or seasonal places are left out; each
place is reduced to five columns (country code, name, latitude, longitude,
population); coordinates are rounded to four decimal places. The script that
does this is scripts/make-places.py.

GeoNames provides the data "as is", without warranty of any kind. GeoNames does
not endorse Waterfall.

places-preferred.tsv is not from GeoNames. It is a hand-made list of name,
position and radius. Each position in it was chosen by hand and then checked
against other maps, and its notes say which: OpenStreetMap (data (c)
OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database Licence,
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), GeoNames (as above) and Wikipedia.
